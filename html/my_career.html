My Career

From Player to Systems Engineer

My journey as a Software Engineer began with an early obsession with how things work under the hood—from rebuilding my PC’s operating system as a kid to asking how my favorite video games were made. Driven by that curiosity, I taught myself the basics of logic and mechanics using Game Maker and Unity. Over the years, this creative passion evolved into a disciplined pursuit of Computer Systems Engineering. Today, I bridge the gap between creative game mechanics and robust, full-stack systems engineering, always coding, learning, and preparing to build the next generation of software.

Mastering the Tech Stack

Pursuing a degree in Computer Systems Engineering gave me a deep appreciation for the mechanics behind complex software. I didn’t just study Java, C++, networking, and databases—I put them to work. I used my passion for interactive media to push the boundaries of my coursework, resulting in highly technical capstones:

  • Networked MMO Prototype: Built a Unity client that successfully communicated with a local backend server to manage character creation, real-time combat, and leveling systems.

  • Level-Sharing Ecosystem: Engineered a "Mario Maker" style prototype integrated with a server and web frontend to dynamically upload and distribute player-created levels.

A Curated Project Library

My portfolio is a curated showcase of deep execution over high volume. As an independent engineer, I use my projects to rigorously test new frameworks, languages, and system designs. Every asset in my library represents hours of troubleshooting, optimization, and refining raw mechanics into polished software. From strict object-oriented design to database integration, these works prove my ability to take a complex idea from a blank text editor to a fully functional application.
